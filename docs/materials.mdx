# Ores & Materials

Fire's Reflamed adds three new ores to the Overworld and a crafted metal tier. Each material has its own processing chain and is used across tools, weapons, and armor.

---

## Titanium

Titanium is the highest-tier ore material in the mod. Mine it, smelt it, and craft it into the best equipment available.

- [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ore) — Found between Y -80 and Y 32
- [](@firesreflamed:deepslate_titanium_ore) — Deeper variant below Y 0
- [](@firesreflamed:raw_titanium) — Raw drop from the ore
- [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ingot) — Smelted from Raw Titanium
- [](@firesreflamed:titanium_nugget) — 1/9th of an ingot
- [](@firesreflamed:block_of_titanium) — Compact storage block

---

## Steel

Steel is a mid-tier crafted metal — there is no Steel ore. It is manufactured from Iron using a two-step process.

- [](@firesreflamed:carbonized_iron) — Combine an Iron Nugget with Coal or Charcoal
- [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot) — Smelt Carbonized Iron to produce Steel
- [](@firesreflamed:steel_nugget) — 1/9th of an ingot
- [](@firesreflamed:block_of_steel) — Compact storage block

---

## Ruby

Ruby is a gem-tier material found in stone layers. It has no armor set — tools and weapons only.

- [](@firesreflamed:ruby_ore) — Found between Y -16 and Y 64
- [](@firesreflamed:deepslate_ruby_ore) — Deeper variant below Y 0
- [](@firesreflamed:ruby_crystals) — Raw drop from the ore
- [](@firesreflamed:ruby_gem) — Craft 2 Ruby Crystals together, or smelt the ore directly
- [](@firesreflamed:block_of_ruby) — Compact storage block; also used for decorative blocks

---

## Aquamarine

Aquamarine is a gem-tier material used exclusively for armor — it has no tool set.

- [](@firesreflamed:aquamarine_ore) — Found between Y -16 and Y 48
- [](@firesreflamed:deepslate_aquamarine_ore) — Deeper variant below Y 0
- [](@firesreflamed:aquamarine_shard) — Drops directly from the ore, no smelting required
- [](@firesreflamed:block_of_aquamarine) — Compact storage block
