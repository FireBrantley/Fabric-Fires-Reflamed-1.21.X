# Fire's Reflamed Wiki

Welcome to the official wiki for **Fire's Reflamed**, a Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21 that expands the game with new ores, metals, weapons, armor, a new wood type, and decorative blocks.

- **Author:** Fire_Brantley
- **Contributor:** piecrusher65
- **Loader:** Fabric (requires Fabric API)
- **Minecraft Version:** 1.21.x
- **License:** MIT
- **Source:** [GitHub](https://github.com/FireBrantley/Fabric-Fires-Reflamed-1.21.X)
- **Modrinth:** [Fire_Brantley's profile](https://modrinth.com/user/Fire_Brantley)

---

## What does this mod add?

Fire's Reflamed adds three new ores, three new material tiers (Titanium, Steel, Ruby), one gem-only armor set (Aquamarine), a brand-new wood type (Willow), a variety of decorative blocks, and unique weapons like **Bats** and the **Drill**.
