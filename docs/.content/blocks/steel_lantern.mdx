# Steel Lantern

The Steel Lantern is a bright light-emitting block crafted from [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot) nuggets and a Torch. It emits a higher light level than a vanilla Lantern and pairs naturally with [](@firesreflamed:steel_chain).

<PrefabObtaining />

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Hardness | 3.5 |
| Light Level | 15 |
| Required Tool | Pickaxe |
| Sounds | Lantern |

## Notes

At light level 15, the Steel Lantern is one of the brightest light sources in the game, matching a full Glowstone block. It can be placed on top of or hanging beneath a solid surface, and connects naturally to [](@firesreflamed:steel_chain).
