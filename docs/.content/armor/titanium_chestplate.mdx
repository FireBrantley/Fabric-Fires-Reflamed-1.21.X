# Titanium Chestplate

The Titanium Chestplate is the chest slot piece of the Titanium armor set. It provides the highest chest protection of any armor in the mod.

## Notes

At 12 defense points, the Titanium Chestplate alone provides more protection than a full Iron helmet + boots combined. See the full set: [](@firesreflamed:titanium_helmet), [](@firesreflamed:titanium_leggings), [](@firesreflamed:titanium_boots).
