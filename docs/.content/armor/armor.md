# Armor

Fire's Reflamed adds four armor sets: **Titanium**, **Steel**, and **Aquamarine**.

## Armor Material Stats

| Material | Total Defense | Enchantability | Toughness | KB Resistance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Titanium** | 30 | 28 | 3.0 | 0.1 |
| **Steel** | 20 | 14 | 0.0 | 0.01 |
| **Aquamarine** | 20 | 19 | 1.0 | 0.01 |

## Obtaining

All sets follow standard vanilla crafting patterns using their respective materials (Titanium Ingots, Steel Ingots, or Aquamarine Shards).
