# Aquamarine Helmet

The Aquamarine Helmet is the head slot piece of the Aquamarine armor set. Aquamarine armor matches Steel in total defense but offers better enchantability and a slight toughness bonus. Aquamarine has no tool set — armor is its only use.

| Steel | 20 | 0 | 14 |
