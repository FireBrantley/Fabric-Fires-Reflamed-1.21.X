# Carbonized Iron

Carbonized Iron is an intermediate crafting material used to produce [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot). It is created by combining an Iron Nugget with any coal variant, then smelted to yield Steel.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Crafting

Combine **1 Iron Nugget** and **1 Coal or Charcoal** (shapeless) to produce 1 Carbonized Iron.

## Processing

Smelt Carbonized Iron in a furnace or blast furnace to obtain [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot):

| Input | Output | XP | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carbonized Iron | [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot) | 0.7 | 200 ticks (furnace), 100 ticks (blast furnace) |

## Notes

Carbonized Iron has no use other than as a smelting input for Steel. It cannot be stacked into a block or used in any other recipe.
