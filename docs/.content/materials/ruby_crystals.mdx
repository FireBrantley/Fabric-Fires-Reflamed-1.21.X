# Ruby Crystals

Ruby Crystals are the raw drop from [](@firesreflamed:ruby_ore) and [](@firesreflamed:deepslate_ruby_ore). Two crystals are crafted together to produce one [](@firesreflamed:ruby_gem), or the ore can be smelted directly.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Obtaining

Mine [](@firesreflamed:ruby_ore) with any pickaxe to receive Ruby Crystals. Deepslate Ruby Ore drops the same item.

## Processing

Craft two Ruby Crystals vertically (shapeless) to produce one [](@firesreflamed:ruby_gem).

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| 2× Ruby Crystals | 1× [](@firesreflamed:ruby_gem) |
