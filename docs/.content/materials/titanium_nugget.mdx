# Titanium Nugget

The Titanium Nugget is a small unit of Titanium obtained by crafting a [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ingot) in a crafting grid. Nine nuggets can be recombined into one ingot.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Obtaining

Place one [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ingot) anywhere in a crafting grid (shapeless) to receive **9 Titanium Nuggets**.

## Uses

Craft 9 Titanium Nuggets in a 3×3 crafting grid to produce one [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ingot).
