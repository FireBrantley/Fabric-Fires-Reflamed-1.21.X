# Steel Ingot

Steel is a mid-tier material, sitting between iron and diamond in most stats. It cannot be found as an ore and must be produced through carbonization.

## Obtaining

1. Combine an **Iron Nugget** + any **Coal/Charcoal** (shapeless) → **Carbonized Iron**.
2. Smelt **Carbonized Iron** in a furnace or blast furnace → **Steel Ingot**.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Usage

Steel is used for tools, armor, bats, and various utility blocks like the **Steel Lantern** and **Drill**.
