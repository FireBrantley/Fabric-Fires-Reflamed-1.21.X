# Steel Nugget

The Steel Nugget is a small unit of Steel obtained by crafting a [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot) in a crafting grid. Nine nuggets can be recombined into one ingot. Steel Nuggets are also used directly in the [](@firesreflamed:drill_bit) and [](@firesreflamed:steel_lantern) recipes.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Obtaining

Place one [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot) anywhere in a crafting grid (shapeless) to receive **9 Steel Nuggets**.

## Uses

- Craft 9 Steel Nuggets in a 3×3 grid to produce one [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot)
- Used in the [](@firesreflamed:drill_bit) recipe
- Used in the [](@firesreflamed:steel_lantern) recipe
