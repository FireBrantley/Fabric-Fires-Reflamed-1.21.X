# Titanium Ingot

Titanium is the highest-tier material in Fire's Reflamed, comparable to diamond in strength but with faster mining speed and high enchantability.

## Obtaining

Titanium Ingots can be obtained by smelting **Raw Titanium** or **Titanium Ore** in a furnace or blast furnace.

<PrefabObtaining />

### Generation

Titanium Ore generates in all Overworld biomes between Y levels **-80 and 32**. It usually generates in veins of up to 10 blocks, with roughly 12 attempts per chunk.

## Usage

Titanium is used to craft high-tier tools, armor, and the Titanium Bat.
