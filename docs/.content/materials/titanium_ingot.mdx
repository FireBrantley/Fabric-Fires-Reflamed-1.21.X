# Titanium Ingot

The Titanium Ingot is the primary crafting material for the mod's highest-tier equipment. It is smelted from [](@firesreflamed:raw_titanium) obtained by mining [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ore).

<PrefabObtaining />

## Obtaining

Smelt Raw Titanium or Titanium Ore in a furnace or blast furnace. Deepslate Titanium Ore takes longer to smelt but yields the same result.

You can also break down a [](@firesreflamed:block_of_titanium) into 9 Titanium Ingots, or combine 9 [](@firesreflamed:titanium_nugget)s into one ingot.

## Uses

Titanium Ingots are used to craft:

- [](@firesreflamed:titanium_pickaxe), [](@firesreflamed:titanium_shovel), [](@firesreflamed:titanium_axe), [](@firesreflamed:titanium_hoe)
- [](@firesreflamed:titanium_sword)
- [](@firesreflamed:titanium_bat)
- [](@firesreflamed:titanium_helmet), [](@firesreflamed:titanium_chestplate), [](@firesreflamed:titanium_leggings), [](@firesreflamed:titanium_boots)
- [](@firesreflamed:block_of_titanium) (9 ingots)

Titanium Ingots also serve as the **repair material** for all Titanium-tier equipment on an anvil.
