# Raw Titanium

Raw Titanium is the raw ore drop from [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ore) and [](@firesreflamed:deepslate_titanium_ore). It must be smelted into [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ingot) before it can be used in crafting.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Obtaining

Mine [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ore) with any pickaxe to drop Raw Titanium. Deepslate Titanium Ore drops the same item. Alternatively, smelt either ore block directly to skip the raw form.

## Processing

| Input | Output | XP | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw Titanium | [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ingot) | 0.7 | 200 ticks (furnace), 100 ticks (blast furnace) |

9 Raw Titanium can also be compacted into a [](@firesreflamed:block_of_titanium) via the standard block compression recipe — but smelting first is more efficient.
