# Titanium Pickaxe

The Titanium Pickaxe is the top-tier pickaxe added by Fire's Reflamed. It mines at diamond level with exceptional speed and durability, and has very high enchantability.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Durability | 2,500 |
| Mining Speed | 13.0 |
| Mining Level | Diamond-equivalent |
| Enchantability | 28 |
| Repair Item | [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ingot) |

> For comparison: Diamond pickaxe has 1,561 durability and 8.0 mining speed.

## Tips

- The high enchantability (28, compared to Diamond's 10) means Titanium tools are excellent candidates for enchanting — you'll get better and more expensive enchantments on average.
- Pair with Efficiency and Unbreaking to make the most of the already impressive speed and durability.
