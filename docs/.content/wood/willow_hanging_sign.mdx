# Willow Hanging Sign

The Willow Hanging Sign is a sign variant that hangs from the underside of blocks using chains. It is crafted from [](@firesreflamed:stripped_willow_log)s and Chains.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Notes

Hanging Signs attach to the underside of a solid block and swing freely. Text can be edited by right-clicking. They differ from the standard [](@firesreflamed:willow_sign) in that they hang rather than stand upright or mount on walls.
