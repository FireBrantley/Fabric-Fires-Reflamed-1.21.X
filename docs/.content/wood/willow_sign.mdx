# Willow Sign

The Willow Sign is a Willow-wood variant of the vanilla Sign, crafted from [](@firesreflamed:willow_planks) and Sticks. A hanging variant, the [](@firesreflamed:willow_hanging_sign), is also available.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Willow Hanging Sign

## Notes

Willow Signs can be placed on the ground or on the side of any solid block. Text and styling follow the same rules as all vanilla signs — use a dye to change text color. Hanging Signs attach to the underside of blocks and swing freely.
