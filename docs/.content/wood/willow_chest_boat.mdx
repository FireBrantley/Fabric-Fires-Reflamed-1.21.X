# Willow Chest Boat

The Willow Chest Boat is a [](@firesreflamed:willow_boat) with a built-in chest, providing 27 inventory slots. It is useful for transporting large quantities of items across water.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Crafting

Combine a [](@firesreflamed:willow_boat) with a Chest (shapeless) to yield 1 Willow Chest Boat.

## Notes

The Willow Chest Boat functions identically to vanilla chest boats — items stored inside are dropped when the boat is destroyed. It can carry one player and opens its inventory when right-clicked while riding.
