# Willow Boat

The Willow Boat is a Willow-wood variant of the vanilla Boat, crafted from [](@firesreflamed:willow_planks). A chest variant, the [](@firesreflamed:willow_chest_boat), is also available.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Uses

The Willow Boat functions identically to the vanilla Oak Boat — it can carry one player and one passenger, floats on water, and breaks when hit with enough force.

Combine a Willow Boat with a Chest (shapeless) to craft the [](@firesreflamed:willow_chest_boat), which has 27 inventory slots and can be used for item transport.
