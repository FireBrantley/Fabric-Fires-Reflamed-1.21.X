# Willow Sapling

The Willow Sapling is a plantable sapling that grows into a full Willow tree. It drops from [](@firesreflamed:willow_leaves) when broken.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Hardness | 0.0 (instant break) |
| Sounds | Grass |
| Light Required | 9+ |

## Growing

Plant a Willow Sapling on Dirt, Grass, Podzol, Coarse Dirt, or Rooted Dirt in an area with at least light level 9. The tree grows naturally over time or can be forced to grow immediately by applying Bone Meal.

A fully grown Willow tree produces: [](@firesreflamed:willow_log)s, [](@firesreflamed:willow_leaves), and [](@firesreflamed:hanging_willow_leaves).

## Notes

Willow Saplings also drop occasionally from [](@firesreflamed:hanging_willow_leaves) blocks when broken, in addition to standard leaf drops.
