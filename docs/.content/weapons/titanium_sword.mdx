# Titanium Sword

The Titanium Sword is the most powerful sword in Fire's Reflamed, crafted from [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ingot). It deals significantly more damage than a Diamond sword and has exceptional enchantability.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Attack Damage | 14.0 |
| Attack Speed | Fast (-2.4) |
| Durability | 2,500 |
| Enchantability | 28 |
| Repair Item | [](@firesreflamed:titanium_ingot) |

> Attack damage is calculated as: material attack bonus (10) + per-item bonus (+4) + base 1 = 14.

## Tips

The Titanium Sword's high enchantability makes it an excellent candidate for applying powerful combat enchantments like Sharpness V or Looting III. Consider pairing with the [](@firesreflamed:titanium_bat) for a dual weapon setup.
