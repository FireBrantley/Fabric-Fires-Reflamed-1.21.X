# Steel Bat

The Steel Bat is a mid-tier bat weapon crafted from [](@firesreflamed:block_of_steel). It is slower than most swords but durable and repairable with [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot).

<PrefabObtaining />

## Stats

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Base Damage Modifier | -2 |
| Attack Speed | Slow (-2.8) |
| Durability | 750 |
| Enchantability | 14 |
| Repair Item | [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot) |

> Final attack damage = Steel material bonus (10) + bat modifier (-2) + 1 = **9**.

## Crafting

```
M M
MMM
_S_
```
- `M` = [](@firesreflamed:block_of_steel)
- `S` = [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot)

## Notes

Bats are **not stackable** (max stack size: 1). Can be enchanted and repaired on an anvil with [](@firesreflamed:steel_ingot). See also: [](@firesreflamed:wooden_bat), [](@firesreflamed:ruby_bat), [](@firesreflamed:titanium_bat).
