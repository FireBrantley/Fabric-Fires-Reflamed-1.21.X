# Bats

Bats are a unique weapon type in Fire's Reflamed. They are melee weapons similar to swords but with different damage and speed trade-offs.

## Variants

| Bat | Material | Base Dmg Modifier | Attack Speed | Repair Item |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![asset](firesreflamed:wooden_bat) **Wooden Bat** | Wood (Stick) | +5 | Slow (-2.9) | — |
| ![asset](firesreflamed:steel_bat) **Steel Bat** | Steel Ingot | -2 | Slow (-2.8) | Steel Ingot |
| ![asset](firesreflamed:ruby_bat) **Ruby Bat** | Ruby Gem | +0 | Medium (-2.7) | Ruby Gem |
| ![asset](firesreflamed:titanium_bat) **Titanium Bat** | Titanium Ingot | +2 | Medium (-2.6) | Titanium Ingot |

## Obtaining

### Wooden Bat Recipe
```
M M
MMM
 _S_
```
- `M` = any log block
- `S` = Stick

### Metal Bats Recipe
Use the same pattern, replacing logs with the respective **material block** (e.g., Block of Titanium) and the stick with the **material ingot** (e.g., Titanium Ingot).
