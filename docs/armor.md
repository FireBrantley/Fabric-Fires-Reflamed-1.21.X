# Armor

Fire's Reflamed adds four armor sets: **Titanium**, **Steel**, **Aquamarine**, and a partial **Ruby** set is not present — Ruby has no armor. Each set uses the standard helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots slots.

---

## Armor Material Stats

| Material | Helmet | Chestplate | Leggings | Boots | Total | Enchantability | Toughness | KB Resistance | Equip Sound | Repair Item |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Titanium** | 4 | 12 | 10 | 4 | 30 | 28 | 3.0 | 0.1 | Diamond equip | Titanium Ingot |
| **Steel** | 3 | 8 | 6 | 3 | 20 | 14 | 0.0 | 0.01 | Iron equip | Steel Ingot |
| **Aquamarine** | 3 | 8 | 6 | 3 | 20 | 19 | 1.0 | 0.01 | Iron equip | Aquamarine Shard |

> For comparison: Iron total = 15, Diamond total = 20, Netherite total = 20 (+3 toughness, +0.1 KB resistance).

---

## Titanium Armor

The best armor in the mod. Higher total protection than diamond and netherite-tier toughness.

**Crafting (standard vanilla patterns using Titanium Ingots):**

| Piece | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Helmet | `III / I_I` |
| Chestplate | `I_I / III / III` |
| Leggings | `III / I_I / I_I` |
| Boots | `I_I / I_I` |

---

## Steel Armor

Mid-tier armor, equivalent to diamond in total protection but with no toughness.

**Crafting (standard vanilla patterns using Steel Ingots):**

| Piece | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Helmet | `SSS / S_S` |
| Chestplate | `S_S / SSS / SSS` |
| Leggings | `SSS / S_S / S_S` |
| Boots | `S_S / S_S` |

---

## Aquamarine Armor

Aquamarine offers the same base defense as steel armor but with better enchantability (19 vs 14) and a slight toughness bonus. It has no associated tool set.

**Crafting (standard vanilla patterns using Aquamarine Shards):**

| Piece | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Helmet | `AAA / A_A` |
| Chestplate | `A_A / AAA / AAA` |
| Leggings | `AAA / A_A / A_A` |
| Boots | `A_A / A_A` |
