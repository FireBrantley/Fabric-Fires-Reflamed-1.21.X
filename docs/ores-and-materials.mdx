# Ores & Materials

Fire's Reflamed adds three new ores that generate in the Overworld, each with its own material tier and uses.

---

## Titanium

Titanium is the highest-tier material in the mod, comparable to diamond in strength but with faster mining speed and high enchantability.

**Obtaining:**
- Mine **Titanium Ore** (found in stone layers) or **Deepslate Titanium Ore** (found in deepslate layers)
- Smelt **Raw Titanium** or **Titanium Ore** in a furnace or blast furnace to get **Titanium Ingots**
- You can also compact/uncompact between **Titanium Nuggets → Titanium Ingots → Block of Titanium**

**Generation:**
- Generates in all Overworld biomes
- Y level: **-80 to 32** (uniform distribution)
- Vein size: up to 10 blocks
- ~12 attempts per chunk

**Smelting:**
| Input | Output | XP | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw Titanium / Titanium Ore | Titanium Ingot | 0.7 | 200 ticks (furnace), 100 ticks (blast furnace) |
| Deepslate Titanium Ore | Titanium Ingot | 0.7 | 300 ticks (furnace), 150 ticks (blast furnace) |

**Uses:** Full tool set, full armor set, Titanium Bat

---

## Steel

Steel is a mid-tier material crafted from iron, sitting between iron and diamond in most stats. It cannot be found as an ore — it must be crafted.

**Obtaining:**
1. Combine an **Iron Nugget** + any **Coal/Charcoal** (shapeless) → **Carbonized Iron**
2. Smelt **Carbonized Iron** in a furnace or blast furnace → **Steel Ingot**
3. Compact/uncompact between **Steel Nuggets → Steel Ingots → Block of Steel**

**Smelting:**
| Input | Output | XP | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carbonized Iron | Steel Ingot | 0.7 | 100 ticks (furnace), 50 ticks (blast furnace) |

**Uses:** Full tool set, full armor set, Steel Bat, Steel Chain, Steel Lantern, Steel Bars, Drill (and Drill Bit)

---

## Ruby

Ruby is a gem material, slightly weaker than steel in durability but with good enchantability. Rubies come in two forms: raw **Ruby Crystals** from mining, and refined **Ruby Gems**.

**Obtaining:**
- Mine **Ruby Ore** (stone) or **Deepslate Ruby Ore** (deepslate) to get **Ruby Crystals**
- Craft 2 Ruby Crystals (vertically) → 1 **Ruby Gem**
- *Or* smelt Ruby Ore directly for Ruby Gems
- Compact/uncompact Ruby Gems into **Block of Ruby**

**Generation:**
- Generates in all Overworld biomes
- Y level: **-16 to 64** (uniform distribution)
- Vein size: up to 8 blocks
- ~14 attempts per chunk

**Smelting:**
| Input | Output | XP | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ruby Ore | Ruby Gem | 1.0 | 200 ticks (furnace), 100 ticks (blast furnace) |
| Deepslate Ruby Ore | Ruby Gem | 1.0 | 300 ticks (furnace), 150 ticks (blast furnace) |

**Uses:** Full tool set (no armor), Ruby Bat, Ruby building blocks (bricks, stairs, slab, wall), Ruby Cluster

---

## Aquamarine

Aquamarine is a gem material used exclusively for armor. It has no tool set.

**Obtaining:**
- Mine **Aquamarine Ore** (stone) or **Deepslate Aquamarine Ore** (deepslate) → **Aquamarine Shards**
- Compact/uncompact Aquamarine Shards into **Block of Aquamarine**

**Generation:**
- Generates in all Overworld biomes
- Y level: **-16 to 48** (uniform distribution)
- Vein size: up to 7 blocks
- ~14 attempts per chunk

**Smelting:**
| Input | Output | XP | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aquamarine Ore | Aquamarine Shard | 0.8 | 200 ticks (furnace), 100 ticks (blast furnace) |
| Deepslate Aquamarine Ore | Aquamarine Shard | 1.0 | 300 ticks (furnace), 150 ticks (blast furnace) |

**Uses:** Full armor set (no tools)
