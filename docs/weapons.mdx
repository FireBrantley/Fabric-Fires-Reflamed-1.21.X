# Weapons

Fire's Reflamed adds swords and a brand new weapon type — the **Bat** — for each material tier. Bats are slower than swords but hit harder, giving you a choice of playstyle at every tier.

---

## Swords

Standard sword weapons, one for each material tier. All swords use fast attack speed (-2.4).

| Sword | Attack Damage | Durability |
|---|---|---|
| [](@firesreflamed:steel_sword) | 6.0 | 750 |
| [](@firesreflamed:ruby_sword) | 5.0 | 650 |
| [](@firesreflamed:titanium_sword) | 14.0 | 2,500 |

---

## Bats

Bats are a new weapon type exclusive to Fire's Reflamed. They deal more damage than swords of the same tier but attack more slowly. Bats are not stackable (max stack: 1) and are crafted using a Block of the relevant material.

| Bat | Attack Damage | Attack Speed | Durability |
|---|---|---|---|
| [](@firesreflamed:wooden_bat) | 7.0 | Slow (-2.9) | Low |
| [](@firesreflamed:steel_bat) | 9.0 | Slow (-2.8) | 750 |
| [](@firesreflamed:ruby_bat) | 11.0 | Medium (-2.7) | 650 |
| [](@firesreflamed:titanium_bat) | 13.0 | Medium (-2.6) | 2,500 |

The [](@firesreflamed:wooden_bat) is available from the very start of the game — no special materials required, just logs and a stick.
