# Willow Wood

The Willow is a new tree type added by Fire's Reflamed. It generates naturally in the Overworld and provides a complete wood set with all the standard block and item variants you'd expect from a wood type, plus unique hanging leaves that give it a weeping willow appearance.

---

## Getting Started

Willow trees generate naturally in the Overworld. Chop one down to get [](@firesreflamed:willow_log)s and collect [](@firesreflamed:willow_sapling)s from the leaves to grow your own. Plant the sapling on any dirt-type block with at least light level 9, or use Bone Meal to speed up growth.

---

## Natural Blocks

Blocks that generate as part of the Willow tree itself.

- [](@firesreflamed:willow_log) — The trunk block. Strip with an axe to get [](@firesreflamed:stripped_willow_log).
- [](@firesreflamed:willow_wood) — Bark-on-all-sides variant, crafted from 4 logs. Strip with an axe to get [](@firesreflamed:stripped_willow_wood).
- [](@firesreflamed:stripped_willow_log) — Stripped trunk block. Used in the [](@firesreflamed:willow_hanging_sign) recipe.
- [](@firesreflamed:stripped_willow_wood) — Stripped bark-on-all-sides variant.
- [](@firesreflamed:willow_leaves) — Leaf canopy block. Drops saplings and occasionally hanging leaves.
- [](@firesreflamed:hanging_willow_leaves) — Decorative hanging foliage that generates beneath the canopy. No collision — players pass right through. Collect with shears.
- [](@firesreflamed:willow_sapling) — Plant to grow a new Willow tree.
